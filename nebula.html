Nebula Forge

In progress Nebula Forge! Find out more about the game down below!

Nebula Forge was an idea which I had, thought deeply about, and now I am wanting to make everything that I ever imagined come to life and for others to enjoy building their own ships and flying through the nebula! The background picture of this page shows a sneak peek of what the game might look like and what we hope to create!

Features

Build Your Ship

Choose parts that affect how your ship looks and performs. Yes, you can make it a burger if you want

Explore Space

Fly through the nebula, avoid asteroids, and don't get killed by your friends/AI!

Battle

Engage in combat against AI pilots or challenge your friends with split screen or with a join code

Unlock More while you play

Play to unlock new parts and upgrades for your ship, your choice!
